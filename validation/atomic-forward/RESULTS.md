# v8 atomic-review forward comparison

This report compares the exact published v7 Skill with the prospectively frozen v8
tools on three independently designed new complex UI tasks. Both arms already have
independent contracts and native observation tools. This is not Skill versus no
Skill, and agreement between AI assessors is not human ground truth.

## Result: more automatic evidence, no established cluster or cost advantage

On identical original raw bytes, v8 adds **498** automatically assessed cells
(484 P, 14 F), with no lost known result or known-result contradiction and no extra
SDK command. Automatic U falls from **732/1944 (37.65%)** to **234/1944 (12.04%)**.
This is evidence for the new rendered-color observation capability. It is not
evidence that the implementations have fewer defects or that native collection is
faster.

The baseline's three missed cells are T3 Android P2 overlap manifestations in
baseline/expanded/submitted. Its original wrapping/clipping discovery already
identified the same cause cluster, and its one repair fixes all six wrapping/overlap
manifestations. Thus **the row-level difference does not establish an extra
independent bug discovered by v8**. The initial defect populations differ: v7 has
three agreed clusters and v8 four, in different independently written sources.
Only T3 gives v7 a nonzero scored D0 defect denominator, versus all three tasks for
v8. The macro recalls of 85% and 100% therefore use one and three defined tasks,
respectively, and are not three matched positive-defect trials. All 18 development
phase spans stay within their prospective upper budgets.
The natural business/control/event failures occur only in the v7 T3 source:
eight control and two event cells, all originally detected and repaired. v8 has no
confirmed initial control/event failure denominator here. This does not demonstrate
reduced business-defect miss rate.

Unresolved alarms need special care. The v7 one and v8 seven T1 grid-column
descriptions are **supported original discoveries outside the deliberately selected
ledger**, not evidence of false product alarms. They describe the same kind of
responsive-grid issue with different state granularity. They cannot be credited
to an unrelated color/wrapping atom or retroactively added to the denominator.
Repairs include grid changes, but this study has no scored grid-repair denominator.
The remaining three v8 alarms concern T2 natural hyphen wrapping: both claim
reviewers find the original claim explicit and supported enough that a false-alarm
label is unjustified, while common D0 quality consensus did not establish a product
F. They remain U. Zero confirmed FP therefore does not mean zero actual false
alarms or prove a lower false-alarm rate. Classical FPR is N/A.

The binding mechanism runs successfully on the selected catalog, but **overall
finding-to-ledger resolution does not improve**. Exact typed descriptors are
17/18 original alarms for v7 and 19/26 for v8; the other 1/7 are the supported
outside-ledger grid descriptions, not seven fabricated expectations. All original
values/units remain unchanged. A generated descriptor prevents value drift; it
does not make an incomplete ledger complete or settle a visual interpretation.

The consumer endpoint differs by only **+2.93 seconds** for v8 on a roughly
175-minute mean. Model phase resource walls sum to 40.54/41.07 minutes across the
three sources; own native resource walls sum to 109.77/113.31 minutes. The full
shared study takes **370.19 minutes from preregistration to reveal**, with 423.01
native command resource minutes and 169.07 independent-judgment task minutes.
Those sums overlap and must not be added as elapsed time. The same-evidence
diagnostic consumes 31.0 process seconds and zero SDK commands. Publication and
the exact-head CI run are additional. There is no demonstrated end-to-end speed
advantage or observed token/money estimate.

## What remains unreliable

All 60 common configurations pass the explicit native environment preflight; the
previous direction/Float32 wiring defects do not gate Android to unknown. Strict
numeric/pixel facts cannot be voted away, and unsupported/missing facts stay U.
These are verified shared infrastructure repairs, not exclusive v8 gains.

Qualitative model interpretation is still unstable. There is one D0 and six FINAL
panel disagreements, all retained. For the unchanged T2 baseline source, the same
fixture/action/bridge hashes and identical complete state-PNG SHA sets receive
D0 P and FINAL U for three wrapping atoms. One FINAL assessor treats the hyphen
break as violating natural wrapping; the other accepts the permitted fallback.
The evaluator exposes that conflict instead of choosing an answer, but **this does
not establish a correct or reproducible visual oracle**. The original three v8
wrapping claims remain unresolved rather than becoming automatic false positives.
[Both opinions and stability evidence](DISAGREEMENTS.md) are preserved.

There are zero confirmed FINAL failures within the selected ledger, **not a
full-brief all-pass**. The final 148 unknown cells include unsupported iOS styles,
coverage limits and visual disagreement. The small 75-versus-73 all-FINAL U
difference is across different implementations and shared collection failures;
it cannot be attributed to v8's collector. The independently supported out-of-ledger
grid issue and the shared acquisition-scope gap below further limit quality claims.

## What changed before the new tasks existed

The new `review_contract.py` turns an independently approved design lock and atomic
review policy into a catalog. The tool generates the assertion identity, expected
value, unit, comparison and provenance. A person or model supplies only the actual
Boolean/unknown observation, reason and original state-frame indices. The checker
reconstructs the bound finding from the original request and raw evidence; changing
an expectation, unit, target, status or binding is rejected. This closes a transport
gap without assuming that the observation itself is correct.

Rendered canvas, notice and action colors are separate atoms. `native_pixels.py`
measures approved target-local regions from original PNGs and native bounds,
retaining the image digest, coordinate mapping, full histogram and support.
Wrapping and overlap have their own qualitative propositions. Missing runtime
SwiftUI font properties remain unknown; this is not a generic computed-style API.

The shared evaluator fixes native environment spellings and verified provider
screen conversion, preserves authoritative numerical facts, separates product
responsibility from strict failure, and combines two fresh independent judgments.
Disagreements remain unknown. These shared repairs cannot be credited as exclusive
v8 quality gains. [Engineering](ENGINEERING.md) and [previous-release analysis](ANALYSIS.md)
describe the implementation and limits.

## Prospective design and execution

The release, metric functions, neutral native evidence and nine shared adapters
were frozen before a separate context authored the new designs. Each design has
approved public requirements, references, fixtures, source-backed expectations and
heldout caller data. Two independent reviews approved the final design before any
product implementation. Rejected design proposals and engineering attempts remain
in the published inventory. No product defect was deliberately injected.

- T1: Workshop Slot Planner, including conflict/selection, expansion and caller
  updates. Its broad workshop theme is shared with an earlier study; its API,
  contract, fixtures and implementation were newly authored after tool freeze.
- T2: River Sensor Triage Board, with multi-state validation and long-content
  display.
- T3: Bicycle Service Intake, with draft revision, selection and submission states.

Each task has two independently produced implementations for both platforms,
normal and narrow/large-font public cases, and a heldout RTL/data combination.
There are seven selected states and 14 selected rules per configuration, including
explicit not-applicable cells. The full brief is broader than the scored ledger.
Stable semantic IDs and new public component APIs are allowed in this experiment.

Initial implementation is limited to 25 minutes, read-only original review to 12
minutes, and one compile-only repair to 15 minutes; early finish is allowed.
All six initial sources seal before any D0 acquisition, all six own D0 corpora and
checks before any original review, all reviews before any repair, all final sources
before any FINAL acquisition, and all own public FINAL raw/check endpoints before
heldout data are materialized. There is no second repair or hidden feedback.

iOS uses real XCUITest/Simulator rendering. Android uses Paparazzi/Compose JVM
rendering and semantic actions, not an emulator or physical touch. Components are
production SwiftUI/Compose files in pinned AlertToast/Reply hosts; bridges decode
inputs and invoke their public APIs. This does not establish arbitrary legacy-app
navigation, backend integration or physical hit testing.

Two fresh quality contexts per task and stage inspect anonymous common-corpus
evidence. Two further fresh claim contexts per task inspect the original self-review
evidence and independently group confirmed product causes. Every complete panel
seals before the next is dispatched. The orchestrating root is privileged and can
read assignments; original finding formats can also reveal tool provenance. This
is assessor isolation, not a double-blind or globally secret experiment.

## Reading the outcomes

[TABLES.md](TABLES.md) is generated from frozen metrics, not hand-entered scores. It
separates machine/manual/combined alarms, original semantic detection, cause
clusters, public and heldout coverage, repair outcomes, costs and exact descriptor
compliance. [summary.json](summary.json) preserves per-task and per-platform values.

A TP requires both independent claim judgments to support the original finding
from its original own evidence and match an independently confirmed common D0
product manifestation. Later repair success does not backfill credit. A schema
rejection or common pass alone does not establish an FP. Unknown claims retain
their possible false-discovery bounds. Classical FPR is unavailable without a
complete independently adjudicated TN universe. Repeated state/platform rows are
not independent defects; cause clusters require exact independent membership
agreement. Unknown is never a successful repair.

## Shared observation and cost limitations

All 108 planned native commands completed with original outputs retained; nine
returned nonzero because of incomplete iOS full-content coverage. A failed sweep
can still contain valid local facts, but cannot prove the whole state is clear.
There are no replacement SDK retries. [FAILURES.md](FAILURES.md),
[FAILURES.json](FAILURES.json) and [GALLERY.md](GALLERY.md) retain the limits and images.
The [qualitative disagreement index](DISAGREEMENTS.md) retains both opinions and
unscored source/launch/state-image stability diagnostics. The shared evaluator
exposes disagreement; it cannot guarantee correct or reproducible model interpretation.

A launch-input audit found a shared harness scope gap: own generated routes have
four after-target observation extensions; common acquisitions use the approved
base routes without those extensions. The business route, fixtures and bridge
otherwise agree. This is preserved without changing frozen routes or recapturing
known cases. Missing common facts remain unknown. Original alarms are checked
against original own evidence, and own/common timing is not a same-route speed
comparison. [The exact audit](action-scope.json) and
[engineering disclosure](ENGINEERING.md#acquisition-scope-gap-found-during-the-frozen-run)
record the issue for future unrelated preflight controls.

The primary consumer endpoint is implementation start to its own FINAL public raw
and automatic-check endpoint, including cohort waits and queues. Raw-only endpoint,
all-six closure, model/compile phase wall, native command resource sums, shared
quality/claim judging and offline replay are separate. Resource sums overlap and
are not elapsed time. The launcher records lock/slot waiting but not a separate
executor submission timestamp; the full endpoint still includes that delay.
Tokens and money were not observed. Same-byte automatic replay adds no SDK commands;
avoiding redundant acquisition is shared infrastructure, not exclusive v8 speed.

Only three task pairs are tested, with different natural initial-defect denominators
and shared hardware/caches. Binding compliance, additional automatic evidence and
end-to-end product quality are different outcomes. This study cannot establish a
general causal improvement in mobile PR quality.
