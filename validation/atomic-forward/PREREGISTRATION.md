baseline v7 is 4db3223a. Previous publications, disputed judgments and failure

approved contract IDs, with expected/unit/operator/provenance generated by tools;

explicit environment aliases, no subjective override of validated numeric/pixel

meaning, disagreement retained as uncertain rather than choosing a favorable label.

synthetic measurement/schema controls and an independent forward pass. No product

public requirements, atomic rule universe, phase budgets and default model settings
