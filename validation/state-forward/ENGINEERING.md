# v5 engineering before Print Batch Approval

## What the previous experiment did not establish

The published v4 trial improved collection cost but did not establish coverage
or business-miss improvements. Its same-source pairs gained two runtime facts,
lost three, and had no assessed disagreements. A common-cause source repair did
not retroactively establish findings at the six missed business states. A last
post-action viewport could still omit relevant distant components. Neither
faster acquisition nor a plausible summary proved complete state coverage.

The original v4 publication is immutable under `validation/publications/`.
Frozen comparison tools, source hashes and actual callback-gate equivalence are
verified by `verify_tools.py`. Historical verification materializes the original
mutable publication aliases rather than changing old manifests or scores.

## Executable capabilities

`observation_contract.py` validates unique exact native selectors, bounded target
sets, shared scroll budgets, unique state identities and action limits before
building a native host. `state_plan.py` compiles a declared state × requirement
matrix into assertions. Every declared intersection needs a sourced expectation
or an explicit reasoned N/A; omitted intersections fail compilation. It can add
the checked native targets to an existing stimulus route while retaining that
route. This checks the declared matrix, not completeness of arbitrary prose.

Both native observers now collect multiple declared targets after an activation,
caller update or checkpoint. They reuse coherent original frames, scroll only
for remaining targets, share one bounded search budget, and record each actual
target result and native snapshot index. Missing and ambiguous targets retain
diagnostics. Searching for evidence does not activate a control a second time.

`native_checks.py` unions target evidence only within the exact action interval,
input, native process and event revision. Conflicting values remain unassessed.
Target coverage never becomes proof of whole-content coverage or bounded
absence. Legacy observations receive only the coverage they actually acquired.
Failed atomic checks export exact state/aspect findings, while original failed
rows from older formats retain equal detection eligibility in the trial.

The successful-dispatch `callback_verdict` function is AST-identical to v4.
Immediate callback receipts close before post-action target collection; later
scrolling does not extend a callback interval. Disabled, ambiguous, missing,
failed or unproven activations do not earn zero-callback credit.

## Neutral validation before the new design

Root and an independent agent each ran actual iOS and Android neutral native
components before the v5 freeze. `generic/`, `generic-evidence/` and
`independent-generic/` retain original native outputs, inputs, sources, images,
timings, negative controls and unsuccessful engineering attempts.

On the same neutral source and inputs, root's iOS assessed rows increased from
7 to 15 of 16, Android from 6 to 15 of 16. The remaining disabled-action behavior
stayed unassessed. Missing-target budget zero stayed unavailable. Target sets
continued to report full-content coverage false. Independent component runs
assessed 14 native rows and two explicitly scoped visual rows per platform;
removing footer evidence reproduced 13 passed / 3 unassessed.

The extra navigation costs time: warm command wall increased 34.226→61.526s
on iOS (+79.762%) and 4.594→5.441s on Android (+18.448%). Cold costs and all raw
frames remain archived. This validation establishes capability and its cost,
not a speed improvement or the outcome of a new business implementation.

79 unit tests and the 37 pre-delivery independent grader controls pass. The
complete prior workflow also passed before business design; its command logs
remain under `generic/prebusiness-ci/`.

## Remaining limits

Public iOS AX is not a generic SwiftUI computed-style inspector. Font, fill,
padding, radius and true physical hit-region requirements remain unassessed
where no actual native measurement exists. Android native text/semantic facts
establish only their exported meanings. Semantic activation does not establish
OS physical touch. Source declarations cannot fill runtime evidence gaps.

These trials call new production components through thin hosts in pinned Reply
and AlertToast projects. They do not establish whole-app navigation, backend
behavior or arbitrary legacy-page discovery without a component bridge.
The new component contract explicitly supplies exact accessibility selectors.
This experiment therefore does not demonstrate reliable ambiguous-prose
localization of arbitrary unchanged legacy views without identifiers.
