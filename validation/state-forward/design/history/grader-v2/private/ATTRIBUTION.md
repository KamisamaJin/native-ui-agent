# Attribution overlay — fixed grades remain literal

This is a separate interpretation layer, fixed before source/raw delivery. It changes no oracle value, native-helper rule, row status, denominator, repair mapping or detection credit.

Retain every frozen literal P/F/U row exactly. A CURRENT label may contradict the planned resulting state and therefore retain its literal F. That alone does not establish a calculation or transition implementation defect if a required earlier stimulus was never demonstrated. Annotate the execution gap and possible downstream scope separately. Continue to assess directly observable static/model-only current labels against the bound current input; do not gate those facts on unavailable activation. A successfully exercised callback defect or transition defect requires its own exact successful requested-stimulus proof.

Use distinct narrative groups:

- confirmed_exercised_business_defect: actual native requested stimulus is established and the failing callback or native resulting transition has direct evidence; list the exact exercised rows.
- confirmed_current_fact_defect: a direct current native contradiction (including static/model-only labels) is observable in the correct input/revision; this does not, by itself, prove a calculation/transition root cause.
- scenario_prerequisite_uncertainty: one or more required earlier activations/updates are unavailable, mismatched or unestablished and could explain a later planned-state contradiction. Retain the literal row and identify the possible dependency.
- execution_dispatch_gap: record actual requested selector, action/state/input mapping, dispatch/helper verdict and immediate interval evidence, with disabled/missing/ambiguous/wrong-target/wrong-input/failed/unknown distinctions where actually supported. A correct disabled action is an unavailable stimulus, not automatically a business defect.
- native_observer_or_session_failure: record acquisition, process, epoch, mapping or viewport failures separately from product defects. Do not infer a business cause from failed collection.

Each attribution annotation contains annotationID, caseID/stateID, referenced literalCellIDs, kind, evidenceRefs, observedFacts, confidence (confirmed/possible/unknown) and optional possibleDownstreamScope. The scope identifies starting gap action/state, subsequent affected states/aspects, a terminating demonstrated reset/replacement/recovery if any, and a reason. It is possible causal scope, not automatic rescore or proof of implementation cause. A selection gap may affect count/summary/charge/balance/approval/priority enablement; a mode gap may affect sheets, row prices and derived totals; a priority gap may affect surcharge/helper/total/balance/approval; expansion affects visibility and toggle state. These are dependencies from the fixed brief, not claims that a particular gap occurred.

A later confirmed replacement/reset can bound earlier possible scope. Actual native recovery can establish later current facts without retroactively proving the missed earlier stimulus or callback. Keep independent simultaneous causes possible. Source-based explanations remain separately identified inferences; a shared-cause repair does not establish prior detection.

The report should state both the literal fixed-row results and the count/scope of confirmed exercised defects versus prerequisite uncertainty. Neither interpretation replaces or selectively removes the other. No v5 benefit is credited for this common evaluator attribution overlay.
