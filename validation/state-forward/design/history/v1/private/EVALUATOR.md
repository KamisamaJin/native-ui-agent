# Frozen evaluation policy — Print Batch Approval

This policy, the public inputs, reference and private acceptance are fixed before either business implementation or any business observation is available. The evaluator has not read either Skill, historical trials/results, product sources or other agents' work. It reads only this trial's preregistration and the explicitly delivered anonymous evaluation batches after freeze. Tool availability is not a reason to alter acceptance.

## Corpus order and blindness

Root collects, assigns and seals corpora. Evaluate only a supplied batch, in this order: both anonymous common-observer D0; both anonymous common-observer FINAL; both original self-reviews; then both same-source D0 v4/v5 observer pairs. Seal the two arms' output for a stage before receiving the next stage. Do not read private assignment information. Do not predict or grade unprovided stages. Preserve raw sources, inputs, observations, mappings, native checks, screenshots, output hashes and evaluated uncertainties. No best-image selection. Recognizable formats limit procedural blindness; report that limit without using it to infer assignments.

## Complete fixed denominator

oracle.json enumerates every cell by stable cellID, platform, caseID, stateID, aspect, class, targetID, operator and expected value. states-ledger.json fixes every initial and post-action state, action index, trigger, full_content flag, model, local state, calculation and immediate event list. Public cases P1-P4 and held-out cases H1-H2 have equal rules; report them separately and together. The corpus has 37 logical states, 74 platform states. The supplied denominator.json is authoritative after validating its sum against oracle.json. No failed, unsupported, offscreen, later-state, disabled-action, held-out or unobserved cell is dropped.

Every cell is P, F or U, except the explicitly enumerated notApplicable.absent cells, which have a reason and form their own fixed non-runtime N/A ledger. Those entries concern typography/text of an element required to be absent; its separate presence=false obligation remains in the business denominator. N/A is never counted as P. Do not manufacture further N/A after observing results. Preserve each row's status, evidence and reason, plus class/platform/public-holdout/case/checkpoint counts.

Report runtime.business.current, runtime.business.stimulus, runtime.style.native, runtime.style.visual, runtime.layout.native, runtime.capability.physicalHit and runtime.coverage separately. stimulus.execution is execution evidence, not business quality. source.api and input.integrity are separate checks and never inflate runtime coverage. Unsupported native styles and physical-hit inspection remain U in their own required classes. Provide P/F/U counts, P/(P+F+U), observed fraction (P+F)/(P+F+U) and conditional pass fraction P/(P+F), with zero conditional denominators N/A. Do not use one aggregate to conceal class differences.

## Current facts versus requested stimulus

A CURRENT fact is a fact natively observable in the named resulting state: text/name, enabled, selected/on, expansion, presence, geometry, or exported native style. It does not require a prior successful activation. Resolve exact target and same input/process/event revision. When a failed/disabled/uncertain action leaves a known coherent current state, score its observed native current facts normally. In particular a disabled approval label and enabled=false can both pass while callback/transition cells remain U. Do not over-gate native facts by an unavailable prior activation. Reusing evidence is permitted only within that same case input, native process and event revision, with no unresolved drift; conflicts are U.

A REQUESTED-STIMULUS callback or transition cell needs demonstrated successful delivery of that exact requested stimulus. Reuse the immutable tool's native_checks.resolve, state_evidence and callback_verdict (or its exact delivered verdict/provenance), not a looser home-grown dispatcher. Activation evidence must identify the unique exact target, correct requested action, one attempt, a successful native dispatch, and the complete immediate callback interval. Do not retry, activate a target twice, extend the interval with observation sweeps, use another target's success, or substitute programmatic source state. A disabled/missing/ambiguous/failed/uncertain action does not establish successful zero callbacks: its callback and transition cells are U. If a defect wrongly enables the control and a real successful dispatch occurs, the enabled=false fact may fail, while that established interval can still be judged against its expected event list. Do not gate behavior on the expected enabled value instead of actual dispatch evidence.

The expected event sequence includes exact count, names, JSON keys/types/values and current model-order job IDs. Ignore JSON object key order only. Extra/missing events fail only within an established complete immediate interval; an incomplete interval is U. The H2 already-selected mode deliberately requires successful dispatch before zero-event credit. A replacement uses the host's successful applied model revision as its requested-stimulus gate, and requires a coherent before/after binding plus its immediate component-callback interval. Reconstructing a fresh component cannot pass the separate source identity check and does not demonstrate production replacement behavior. Runtime replacement facts can still be scored as current facts if observed.

A transition cell requires the successful gate and actual native resulting facts for model-dependent labels, selected IDs, mode, priority, expansion and total. A matching source implementation, callback payload or declared expected state cannot stand in for a native transition. A complete callback can pass while transition is U if native results are unobserved, and the reverse CURRENT facts may pass while the gated transition stays U.

## Native evidence, units and generic precision

Use actual observations and retain original numbers, units, density/scale and measurement uncertainty. Platform logical units are Android dp for bounds, sp for font sizes; iOS pt for bounds and font size. Convert physical screenshot pixels only with the recorded native density/scale. No guessed conversion. Content scroll offsets are retained; comparisons use matching coordinate space. Screenshot visual review and numerical native checks are independent.

The following generic tolerances are fixed now, without seeing a result. Scalar font-size equality uses epsilon 0.75 sp/pt. Other measured logical scalar/position equality uses epsilon 2 dp/pt. A raw exported native scalar has uncertainty 0 unless the observer declares otherwise. A coordinate from native reported bounds has uncertainty 1 dp/pt; a dimension formed by subtracting two uncertain edges has uncertainty 2 dp/pt. A screenshot-derived edge has uncertainty max(1 pixel / recorded density, 0.5 logical units); a derived dimension sums its two edge uncertainties. Report the actual interval and tolerance. Equality P requires the entire uncertainty interval within expected±epsilon; F requires it disjoint; intersection without containment is U. Minimum 48 bounds: P if the entire interval is >=48; F if the entire interval is <48; otherwise U. Minimum bounds have no extra epsilon that weakens the threshold. Do not round before comparison or fit tolerances later.

Exact exported ARGB colors compare all channels exactly after canonical #RRGGBB -> opaque #FFRRGGBB conversion. No screenshot anti-aliased pixel is treated as an exact font/color inspector. Font weight requires a real exported native weight; size evidence alone cannot pass it. Text compares exact Unicode string with only platform line-break layout normalized to spaces where the string itself is unchanged; do not normalize away punctuation, copy counts, locked suffixes or prices. Boolean semantic values may use framework equivalents described by API.md; unknown/missing attributes are U rather than inferred from label, color or source.

Android Compose may provide TextLayout font_sp, color_argb and lines, native semantic actions and touch_bounds. These establish only their exported meanings. It does not establish an OS physical touch region. iOS publicAX can establish its reported text, values, enabled state and bounds; it does not establish generic SwiftUI font/padding/color or physical hit-region. Numerical style tokens absent from a real native inspector remain U on either OS. Native bounds cannot establish an internal SwiftUI padding token or corner radius. Neither code declarations nor screenshots establish numerical native styles. Both OS physicalHitRegion cells remain U without a separate genuine physical-hit inspection capability. Semantic bounds may still be graded on their own evidence.

## Screenshot review and coverage

Expected visual meanings require inspection of actual native screenshots tied to the named state. A declaration, source token, golden comparison claim or unviewed image never passes a screenshotReview cell. Inspect all provided frames, preserving their order and scroll context; do not select only favorable frames. Score atomic visual rows independently. Where all relevant content is not visible across available frames, the row is U unless an actual visible violation proves F. An overlong label can fail visible truncation even if other content is unobserved; unobserved text cannot be assumed clipped. A visible enabled/disabled style is not proof of native enabled state.

A full_content checkpoint requests the whole scrollable content, potentially several frames. completeCoverage passes only if all current required exact targets are uniquely observed and absence requirements have a completed bounded content search at that revision. Non-full checkpoints retain the same cell universe; lacking evidence is U. Observation sweeps may scroll to collect remaining exact targets but cannot create extra stimulus or change local state. Collapse absence must be established over the covered content/tree, not inferred from one screenshot. A missing target after proven complete bounded content coverage can fail a presence=true obligation; otherwise missing remains U. Render-time crashes or input divergence remain explicit, without replacing every unobserved fact with F.

## Source and input checks

Read only the supplied anonymous production capsule for the stage. Source review checks exact public APIs, designated path, bridge import/call and the caller's preserved component identity. It may identify source risks with file/line evidence, but cannot turn an unsupported runtime cell P or F by inference. Report source findings separately from observed native defects. Root host/evaluator/publication repairs are separately attributed; they do not become observer improvements.

Validate input membership and wiring separately: input.schema.json, complete model values, host settings, action sequence, one-attempt action mapping and exact state mapping. Root supplies mapping.json keyed by stateID, with input_index, trigger_action_index, state_action_index and state_id. Trigger action indexes bind behavior intervals; resulting state action indexes bind state evidence. Initial action index is 0 logically. A full-content action may map native `.trigger` then sweep stateID; the sweep never extends the callback interval. A mismatch is input.integrity F/U, not a product defect; coherent native facts are retained with their actual context and cannot be relabelled as the requested case.

## Self-review matching and repair attribution

Keep original reviews and actual original native check reports sealed. Parse every claimed finding, including prose/manual findings and rows without a machine_findings field. In particular a v4 failed row receives the same matching credit as an equivalent v5 failed row; machine_findings is an output format, not an eligibility rule. Preserve uncertain, unmapped and duplicate claims in the report. Never discard them to improve precision.

For every natural D0 runtime F row, record manual-only detection and combined actual typed-check+manual detection separately. A typed checker finding needs a real matching failed check in the delivered native report, state, target and aspect; a planned check or generated declaration is insufficient. Matching uses the actual failing requirement at its state, not merely a guessed shared cause. Mark exact, partial/uncertain or unmapped. A shared-cause repair does not retroactively prove detection. Source-only risks stay separately mapped and do not count as native detection.

Fixed-row repair evaluates each original D0 F cell against FINAL as F->P, F->F or F->U, preserving all losses as well as gains elsewhere. Natural F count zero gives repair denominator N/A, never 100%. For detection coverage report confirmed exact matches / original natural F rows, with uncertainty counts beside it. For false alarms use the explicitly conditional false-discovery share: confirmed false claims / all adjudicable positive claims; report unmapped/uncertain claims separately and also a conservative interval treating them both ways. Do not call this classical FPR or use FP/(FP+TN) without a separately enumerated complete true-negative universe. A manual-only claim is not a machine finding.

## Normalized evidence and output

NORMALIZATION.schema.json is an interchange contract, not a substitute for raw evidence. Root may supply raw observation.json, mapping.json and immutable native-helper output; an adapter records each exact cell's native value, evidence kind/references/context, helper verdict and stimulus provenance. Preserve all helper uncertainties. The grader may automate arithmetic, string/boolean/native-scalar comparisons and summary counts after checking provenance. Manual screenshot and source review must be explicit reviewed records. Missing records default U. Duplicate conflicting records are U, never last-write-wins. Each output cell retains cellID, class, expected, actual, P/F/U/N/A, reason, evidenceRefs and gate result. No stage is graded until explicitly supplied.
