Before-snapshot binding and after-binding do exist elsewhere in these raw observations, but joining them into a replacement dispatch envelope would change the frozen evidence contract and was not done. This is a common provenance-format/evaluator-order limitation; it establishes neither a wrong model nor a handler/product error. Why that producer envelope was omitted beyond the observed blocked-step format is not established here.

That raw fact must be reported separately from the two different frozen U reasons: neither group proves post-activation behavior or a zero-callback success.
