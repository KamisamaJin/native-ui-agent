# Independently frozen evaluator preparation

No candidate source, candidate evidence, previous trial, result, generic probe,
tool Skill, or assignment was read. Preparation read only the original sealed
design and the explicitly authorized shared contract.py, native_checks.py and
OPERATOR.md. The named native_bridge_format.py was not found and was not needed.

Run the evaluator with an anonymous manifest:

~~~sh
python3 evaluator.py grade --manifest /absolute/capsule/manifest.json --output /absolute/new-report.json
~~~

Report paths must be fresh. normalized-d0.json and normalized-final.json retain
all expected JSON exactly; their adaptation only supplies the shared gate's field
names and the three frozen method translations. Runtime assertions, immutable
input-member provenance and source/API assertions have separate denominators.
Missing native capabilities, observations, manual decisions or source review
produce explicit U rows, rather than disappearing.

Primary business misses are confirmed runtime business F rows after a demonstrated
successful requested activation or caller replacement. The primary post-stimulus
required scope includes all such frozen rows, including U caused by missing or
blocked stimuli. Report the established subset and P/F/U separately. Initial
native/input-derived business observations are a wider descriptive scope, never
silently included in the primary confirmed-stimulus miss count. Input-member and
source/API cells never inflate runtime coverage.

The phase barrier and manual interpretation protocol are in manual-protocol.md.
Concrete artifact and decision formats are in decision-format.md and the schemas.
dependencies.json pins the unchanged shared gate and authorized operator protocol.

negative-controls.py uses synthetic process/input/control records and a completely
missing corpus. It tests common gate success/failure, wrong routes, blocked/disabled
actions, exact selector binding, post-stimulus business gates, callback contradictions,
unsupported native capabilities, row omissions/duplicates, bucket substitution,
and preservation of every expected value. No business implementation is involved.

PREP-SEAL.json is an initial pre-candidate preparation commitment. Later purely
administrative raw schema adapters may be versioned and preserved as separate
seals, with their difference and reason recorded; they may not alter expected
requirements, gates, decision criteria, or previously frozen phase results.
