# Required qualitative final review, before any implementation

The independently authored new design has two qualitative aspects (wrapping and overlap). Acquisition alone leaves those exact cells U. Each repair participant therefore performs a source-closed, image-backed final actuals review after its fresh full-public capture, before the all-self-submissions barrier and hidden execution. No production edit is allowed in this review; any final finding remains failed/unassessed rather than being silently repaired after closure.

Both conditions use the existing review_contract catalog/bind and native_checks evaluator with original lock/policy/image hashes. Participants submit actual observations, original snapshot indices and reasons, never expected values or units. The common operator reconstructs and retains the bound manual report, evaluated native report and admission. Original acquisition-only endpoints and raw automatic U are preserved. A separately recorded full-self endpoint ends after this required review and is used for repair_endpoint_seconds; successful delivery still additionally needs independent hidden/quality agreement. External research panels remain separately costed.

This is execution of the frozen protocol's full-public final review, not a change to the frozen core/metric algorithms. In particular, the default planner's handling of pending qualitative observations remains unchanged and is included in measured cost. No manual P is injected into a capture receipt, and incomplete native coverage cannot be filled by a source declaration.
