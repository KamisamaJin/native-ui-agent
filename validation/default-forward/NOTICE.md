The production capsules include unchanged pinned AlertToast and Reply sources. Each capsule's licenses/ folder preserves AlertToast's MIT and Reply's Apache-2.0 texts; original source headers remain unchanged. See the repository THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md for upstream revisions and dependency licenses. Native screenshots/reports document these integrations and do not relicense upstream assets, Apple/Android SDKs, fonts or dependencies, or imply endorsement.

Full upstream checkouts, Java/Android/Xcode runtimes, simulator images, build caches, signing material and credentials stay in ignored local cache. Only portable source, exact inputs, original observation/image evidence, compiler/native logs and sealed study records are published. Source/API compatibility checks do not establish old-OS packaging/link/runtime behavior.

Source/API emitted module products already named in explicit cohort seals are retained as compiler evidence. Native manifest build-cache product hashes record identities observed at execution time; they do not promise publication of SDK/build caches or permit fresh binary validation without rebuilding.
