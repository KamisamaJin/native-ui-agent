  10 its single aspect/method, and the component/host/system responsibility of its
  11 logical target. Visual Boolean propositions come from exact typed source pointers
  12 in the same sealed design, so `actual:true` answers an explicit approved question.
  13 Rendered color atoms use opaque source-backed ARGB. A canvas rule must specify
  14 which layer paints the sampled region; an ambiguous owner is a design issue to
  15 resolve before scoring. Hashes do not establish design correctness or authorship.

  65 ## What rendered-color evidence establishes
  66 
  67 Pixel rules operate automatically on every eligible coherent state frame. The
  68 independent sampling policy fixes a normalized rectangle inside a uniquely
  69 resolved native semantic/control box, minimum support and pixel count, explicit
  70 RGB channel tolerance, and fully-visible versus visible-fragment scope. The
  71 tool recomputes native-to-PNG mapping, clips to the actual viewport, and records
  72 the original PNG SHA256, native selector/box, pixel region and full histogram.
  73 It never accepts AI-entered observed RGB values or chooses samples by closeness
  74 to the expected color. RGB tolerance is not distance between packed ARGB integers.
  75 
  76 The assertion concerns the dominant rendered sRGB in that approved region. A
  77 region meant to measure solid fill must be independently suitable for that role;
  78 glyph ink, an overlaid child or a gradient may make it uninformative. Insufficient
  79 support, conflicting values, missing mapping or unsupported color profiles stay
  80 unknown. An eligible visible frame with unavailable measurement blocks a pass even
  81 if another frame matches; an explicit new native viewport packet never falls back
  82 to legacy geometry when its node mapping is missing. State/input identities and
  83 after-capture epoch bindings must agree for all accepted evidence. Transparent expectations need a separate approved compositing model.
  84 This is not a generic computed SwiftUI font/color/padding API, whole-canvas proof,
  85 or physical hit-area measurement. Existing unsupported native-style cells remain.

   2 
   3 Use this when selected business states require several independent native facts,
   4 especially after a caller replacement or when related targets are far apart.
   5 The caller agent chooses relevant states and translates the independent design.
   6 The compiler checks that declared matrix; it cannot establish that prose has
   7 been translated completely or that a supplied expectation/source is correct.

   9 ## Compile before feedback
  10 
  11 Write a matrix with `version:1`, `states`, `requirements`, `required_state_ids`
  12 and `required_requirement_ids`. Every selected state × requirement becomes a
  13 cell. A requirement names its source and an atomic native check. A constant
  14 `check.expected` applies across selected states; `by_state` can override expected,
  15 target, action_index, stimulus, required_method or op. Without a default, every
  16 applicable state needs its own expected value. `not_applicable` maps state IDs to
  17 nonempty reasons; those cells remain in the ledger for independent review.
  18 
  19 ```json
  20 {
  21   "version":1,
  22   "required_state_ids":["after-request","after-replacement"],
  23   "required_requirement_ids":["status","action-enabled"],
  24   "states":[
  25     {"id":"after-request","input_index":0,"after_action":0,"coverage":"targets"},
  26     {"id":"after-replacement","input_index":1,"after_action":1,"coverage":"targets"}
  27   ],
  28   "requirements":[
  29     {"id":"status","source":"Independent public status contract",
  30      "check":{"kind":"control","target":{"identifier":"actual.status"},
  31               "property":"label","expected":"Ready"},
  32      "by_state":{"after-replacement":{"expected":"Paused"}}},
  33     {"id":"action-enabled","source":"Independent enablement contract",
  34      "check":{"kind":"control","target":{"identifier":"actual.action"},
  35               "property":"enabled","expected":true},
  36      "by_state":{"after-replacement":{"expected":false}}}
  37   ]
  38 }
  39 ```
  40 
  41 ```sh
  42 python3 scripts/state_plan.py --matrix matrix.json --actions original-actions.json \
  43   --output fresh-plan
  44 ```
  45 
  46 `plan.json` contains every applicable atomic check and required ID, plus a
  47 SHA-bound declared matrix/disposition ledger. `actions.json` adds exact state
  48 names and deduplicated `after_targets` from relevant checks. It preserves action
  49 routes and callback requests; it adds no business expectation to the observer.
  50 The output directory must be fresh. Original action names that disagree with
  51 the matrix are rejected. A state can use `stimulus_action` to identify an earlier
  52 requested stimulus separately from its explicit full-sweep observation action.
  53 
  54 Choose actual producer state IDs and action indices. Initial and explicit sweep
  55 states may use `coverage:"full_content"`. Other actions use `coverage:"targets"`.
  56 Compilation does not insert full sweeps or establish missing native capabilities.
  57 Preserve the matrix and plan before reviewing rendered feedback; changing an
  58 expectation later needs a separate requirement reason, not a better test score.
  59 
  60 ## Collect and judge
  61 
  62 Run the native observer with the generated actions, then `native_checks.py` with
  63 the generated plan. The observer records the immediate action result and original
  64 callback interval, then observes each target. Native frames can jointly establish
  65 facts for targets that cannot fit in one viewport. All frames must have the exact
  66 same native input/process/event binding. Input drift, wrong state/action, torn
  67 image/hierarchy captures and conflicting values remain U.
  68 
  69 Only actual matching native facts earn a verdict. The producer's
  70 `all_targets_observed:true` flag alone is not evidence. Unseen targets cannot prove
  71 absence without complete native full-content coverage. An oversized target may
  72 have its center observed while full visibility remains false. Joint target frames
  73 never establish complete content, effective padding/fonts or physical hit areas.
  74 
  75 Callbacks still require demonstrated requested dispatch. Current labels and
  76 enabled/selected native facts are separate observations; a label after a blocked
  77 action is not proof that its handler executed or that zero callbacks passed.
  78 
  79 The check report retains all failed and unassessed IDs and exports
  80 `machine_findings` for failed typed-native checks, one record per actual state and
  81 aspect. Preserve this report with its observation hash in the agent's review.
  82 Keep manual visual findings separate; duplicate root causes can be grouped for
  83 repair while their distinct state checks remain visible. A later shared repair
  84 does not retroactively establish that another state was reviewed or detected.
  85 
  86 Multi-target navigation costs additional work. Limit targets to the independently
  87 planned aspects relevant to that state and retain the shared scroll budget,
  88 unavailable targets, full-state coverage and command wall time. Do not shrink
  89 content, bypass required states or drop U rows to improve coverage or speed.
  90 
  91 iOS lazy containers can revise their estimated total height while scrolling.
  92 Version 2 collection retains the original estimates and full frames. If the
  93 existing stable-height interval proof is insufficient, `scroll_coverage.py`
  94 requires a top-to-terminal strip of exact overlapping original pixels plus two
  95 unambiguous, vertically distinct native leaf landmarks at every link. Landmark
  96 traits, projected positions, viewport mapping and the process/input/event epoch
  97 must remain coherent. Container IDs and repeated labels cannot supply identity.
  98 The host-owned scroll indicator is hidden at rendering time in this deterministic
  99 fixture; no original pixels are masked, resampled or given a difference tolerance.
 100 
 101 Native CGRect doubles keep their exact represented rational values. The fixed
 102 `native-rect-pixel-cell-enclosure-v1` model constrains positive integer rectangle
 103 possibilities through every edge and the original width/height. Exact full-width
 104 raster comparison selects a unique integer translation; geometry is never
 105 rounded into a different style value. Whole-state repeated identities and their
 106 native order must remain compatible. Terminal coverage requires the executed
 107 native sentinel hittability guard and actual contained root-member geometry.
 108 
 109 Fallback also requires a pinned `coverage_domain_ref` and `coverage_provider_ref`.
 110 They independently approve the finite source revisions and actual shared host
 111 shape. The tool matches the installed production subject/bridge, original native
 112 compile action/log and actual compiled-host bytes. An unused matching source copy
 113 or a self-entered shape label cannot grant coverage. Unapproved source revisions
 114 produce a default `review_source_domain` task before additional SDK work. Separate
 115 source approvals under the same immutable domain/oracle policy cannot waive a
 116 source change, repair completion or fresh native recheck.
 117 
 118 The receiver checks the actual runtime viewport, environment and native display
 119 scale against that domain. It checks the original PNG header before decoding;
 120 16-bit or unsupported encodings cannot be silently converted into an 8-bit pass.
 121 One bounded global rectangle assignment must preserve every link's original
 122 disjoint witnesses and whole-state native order; local pair choices alone do not
 123 establish a full chain.
 124 
 125 Presence is existential over the coherent bound state. A lazy node missing from
 126 one viewport does not contradict its later observed presence. Absence needs a
 127 complete sweep with no match anywhere. An observed duplicate establishes native
 128 nonuniqueness and is not cancelled by a later viewport containing one match.
 129 All original per-frame observations remain attached to the quantified result.
 130 Positive full-state uniqueness also requires complete native matching-domain
 131 coverage. A fully enumerated target query can establish only local uniqueness;
 132 its `complete:false` and query scope remain visible. A lone reported flat node
 133 does not establish query completeness. Boolean/float aliases cannot substitute
 134 for the protocol's integer input, action or snapshot references. An explicit
 135 complete empty target query can decide local absence without a whole-state
 136 sweep; it cannot prove whole-state absence.
 137 
 138 Native query cardinality is checked against its emitted format. Explicit query
 139 resolutions must be recognized. Structured trees need one connected, acyclic
 140 native identity graph, and Android's original semantics geometry must retain its
 141 version, pixel unit, density, position and integer size. Repeated records with the
 142 same native identity are malformed evidence, not multiple controls. Conflicting
 143 group counts, native nodes, identities or visibility remain unassessed with the
 144 original query facts attached; they do not create a product failure.
 145 
 146 `present` and `unique` quantify the actual native matching set, including an
 147 offscreen native entity. They do not assert visibility or activation readiness.
 148 Those facts retain their separate original flags, geometry and action guards.
 149 
 150 Missing originals, unsupported mappings, moving content, sparse or ambiguous
 151 links and unapproved domains remain unassessed. The proof describes the observed vertical
 152 viewport strip. It does not certify hidden branches, arbitrary asynchronous data,
 153 computed styles or business correctness. Coverage reconstruction requires the
 154 actual original image root, including when diagnosing or routing failed checks.
