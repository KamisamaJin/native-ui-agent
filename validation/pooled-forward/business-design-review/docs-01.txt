   6 # Native UI Agent
   7 
   8 在用户已有的 coding agent 中运行本 Skill。由 agent 读图、推理和修改源码；随附 runner 执行已确认的渲染命令、保存证据并汇总检查。runner 本身不调用模型。可选的 [iOS observer](assets/ios-observer/CONTRACT.md) 能生成薄 App/XCUITest 宿主，调用方仍需提供连接真实生产组件的桥接文件。
   9 
  10 ## 接入实际工程
  11 
  12 先确认本次仓库、平台、UI 框架、组件入口、构建版本和已有渲染能力。只读目标平台的 [平台适配](references/platforms.md)。保留旧项目的框架和部署版本，优先复用同类组件、主题、字体、资源及现有测试。
  13 
  14 若与工作区的 mobile-native-delivery 一起使用，由 delivery 负责业务路径、数据和生命周期验收，本 Skill 负责视觉实现与修正；本 Skill 也可以独立使用。
  15 
  16 按本次改动定位组件，不要求全项目登记组件或批量添加业务 View ID。结合变更文件、调用位置、页面文案和实际渲染验证目标。访问受限或依赖复杂时，从真实父容器或实际页面创建，明确测试范围。
  17 
  18 先验证本次依赖的观测能力，再评价产品：实际外观、可用宽度、字体类别、方向与输出配置一致；目标测试确实执行。涉及交互或长内容时，用独立简单探针确认真实点击回调、调用方更新和滚动采样。宿主契约错误单列，不把错误环境里的截图直接归因为产品缺陷。
  19 
  20 涉及原生控件、长内容或调用方状态替换时，使用[原生工具](references/native-tools.md)：`ios_observe.py` / `android_observe.py`采集原生层级关系及逐状态完整滚动证据，`native_checks.py`执行绑定实际输入/动作的原子断言。先读联合控件事实再判断包装节点；拒绝多叶节点自动点击。自动断言与视觉判断分别保留，未测方面不能被整条规则的“通过”覆盖。
  21 
  22 回调行为必须先通过工具的成功派发与指定方法门控；`blocked_disabled`、未找到目标或仅有空事件区间都不能判为业务通过或失败。动作后按当前原生几何重新观察目标，保留即时帧、定位后帧及状态绑定；视口仍缺目标时保留未评估。可复用已验证的编译产物，不能复用旧输入、进程或截图冒充新证据。
  23 
  24 复杂交互涉及多个位置时，使用[逐状态证据计划](references/state-evidence.md)：把本次选定状态与检查方面编译为原子矩阵，为每个动作声明 `after_targets`。工具保留同一输入/事件版本中的多目标帧，并输出逐状态机器发现；缺失预期和没有理由的不适用项在采集前拒绝。联合目标帧不等于完整页面扫描；同根因修复不能反向填补原本未检查的状态。
  25 
  26 ## 固定期望与最小状态集
  27 
  28 从 testcase、设计、既有已批准基准和项目先例提取期望，并保留出处。新设计参考与既有界面回归分别记录。把推断标注为假设；无法确定期望时保留待确认项。
  29 
  30 新增 UI 要在实现前固定独立设计参考和验收标准。线框图用于结构、层级及明确标注的样式意图；不要拿实现后的截图当作其自身的设计参考，也不要把不同渲染器的线框字体差异当成像素回归。能从原生布局/文字结果获得的关键数值单独断言；图中明确的颜色或样式细节也需逐项核对。
  31 
  32 新增或显著改变样式时，使用[独立设计契约](references/design-contract.md)：由独立设计上下文先固定带具体出处和原生单位的值、逐状态预期与动作路径，再封存契约并在实现之外记录批准摘要。`design_contract.py` 生成样式及业务断言；实现方只绑定实际节点。`native_checks.py` 使用独立摘要重建校验生成计划，拒绝预期、容差或覆盖的静默改写。哈希证明身份，不能证明自然语言理解正确或作者独立；不清晰的设计仍需独立审查。
  33 
  34 需要可复算的视觉发现时，使用[原子审查与契约绑定](references/atomic-review.md)：先把配色、画布、换行、重叠分别固定为单方面规则和明确责任，`review_contract.py` 生成发现身份、预期、单位及出处。人工仅提交实际观察和原始帧，不能重写预期；原始像素按独立策略自动采样。独立验收可用 `acceptance.py` 校验真实环境并保留两份独立视觉判断的分歧；严格数值失败、确认产品缺陷与报告格式错误分开记录。
  35 
  36 复杂改动使用[需求决策与定向反馈](references/decision-feedback.md)：`requirement_routes.py` 将独立确认的必需需求逐项关联到验证路径。缺测、契约遗漏、环境错误和判据分歧分别触发后续行动；必需项没有可用验证路径时不能宣告交付通过。已确认索引是否完整仍需独立审查。
  37 
  38 使用 `execution_plan.py` 从同一独立计划生成动作、动作后观测和覆盖范围，并在实际采集前重建校验。先做本次修改相关状态的快速反馈，再做新源码的完整最终验收；定向采集保留未评估范围，不替代完整交付。iOS 可合并同一新鲜状态扫描内的目标观测，保留真实点击、即时帧和版本绑定。
  39 
  40 选择本次相关的普通内容、长文本、缺省内容、主题、字体或尺寸场景，不做所有维度的笛卡尔积。每份 profile 对应一个平台、组件状态和一张实际图片。两端分别渲染、分别比较参考图，共享业务规则。
  41 
  42 数值几何检查使用[原生精度与封存屏障](references/native-precision.md)：保留实际原生像素尺寸/density和逐帧来源，避免从舍入端点相减；不增加默认 epsilon 或放宽设计容差。多候选盲测先固定全体成员并用 `cohort_gate.py` 强制完整封存后再启动采集；单项目日常开发无需建立试验组。
  43 
  44 复杂需求在实现前建立“需求 × 场景 × 检查方面”的证据计划，区分布局、字体、颜色、状态、交互和无障碍。拆开一条规则中可独立缺证据的方面，例如可见标签与实际点击。按 [覆盖与运行时观测](references/coverage.md)记录依据；`scripts/coverage.py`核验缺项和证据指纹，不能替代原生判定。无需为简单改动扩展无关矩阵。
  45 
  46 按 [执行与证据协议](references/protocol.md)为项目生成真实 profile。随附 [Android 示例](assets/profile.example.json)和 [iOS 示例](assets/profile.ios.example.json)展示字段结构，需要替换为当前项目确认的命令、路径和期望。必需检查在实现前固定；改变检查时保留需求依据。
  47 
  48 ## 默认原生反馈入口
  49 
  50 新工作流先读[有界设备池、平台失效与原包复用](references/pooled-workflow.md)。默认入口自行传递历史收据和修复交接，按真实平台依赖决定失效；同一 PR 可复用自有模拟器，每次原生采集仍重置宿主并取得新会话。独立批准的有效性条件允许完整原包用于最终门控时，保留原始身份并另做资源收尾；明确要求新采集时使用 `--fresh`。
  51 
  52 复杂原生组件开发由[默认执行服务](references/default-feedback.md)接入 `ui_loop.py native`；集成端每轮自动读绑定源码/原图的审查注册表，调用路由、定位诊断与复验规划。待看图、修源码、测量争议与实际原生采集按任务类型分流；最终新图审查用原包闭环，无需再采集。禁止手选状态绕过该链路。保留服务 trace、开放任务、原始包与完整最终门控。下面旧 profile 的 capture/review 仅用于兼容既有简单项目。
  53 
  54 执行前读取[资源、动作守卫与证据责任](references/managed-execution.md)。默认服务登记并限制原生临时资源，自动回收自建设备，在最终采集/异常时清理自有编译缓存；借用资源只计量不删除。独立动作依赖和原生前置条件由两端执行器在派发前检查，失败路径保留 U 并阻断无效后续动作。源文件、桥接和集成要求通过独立责任契约检查证据类型/实际文件边界；源码实际值审查进入同一交付门控，不能借组件文件替桥接或运行时要求作证。

  56 ## 实现并取得反馈
  57 
  58 1. 先记录相关构建和原有视觉基线。
  59 2. 在生产组件上做本次需要的改动，复用已有样式与布局方式。
  60 3. 复用或生成薄的测试宿主，直接调用生产组件，注入确定性数据和主题。
  61 4. 用 scripts/ui_loop.py capture 执行 profile，读取 capture.json、原生测试报告及实际图片。进程成功和 captured 都不是样式通过。
  62 5. 阅读完整图片和必要的局部裁剪。结合组件调用、布局数据和测量证据定位问题。
  63 6. 用 scripts/ui_loop.py review 执行数值、像素检查并合并 agent 的视觉 findings，输出 report.json。
  64 
  65 测试宿主需要调用真实实现。对网络、时间、随机数、远程图片和动画使用工程已有的可控制入口。复制一份近似 UI 会使测试失去与生产组件的联系。
  66 
  67 数值结论使用测试宿主产生的测量证据。截图只能支持视觉判断，不能证明所有实际字号、颜色或 padding 属性。UIKit/View 属性、几何边界、文字绘制边界分别说明。SwiftUI/Compose 无法读取的属性保留 needs_review，不由 agent 填造数值。
  68 
  69 “完整”要有采样记录：内容超出视口时保留带重叠的滚动图片及实际底部/目标可见证据；到不了的部分保留未评估。需要 iOS 运行时节点或操作时，切换实际 App + XCUITest，不能用单测宿主的空 AX 或源码声明替代执行。随附 iOS observer 是薄宿主模板，仍需接入真实组件并先运行能力探针。
  70 
  71 ## 按证据修正
  72 
  73 用户要求实现或修复时，记录“失败场景 → 证据 → 源码位置 → 原因假设 → 最小修正”，修正后重新 capture 和 review。纯测试请求输出发现，不自动修改生产代码。
  74 
  75 用 `retest_plan.py` 将原始失败与独立批准的回归依赖转换成复验状态；检查范围不明时执行完整计划。让现有修复记录驱动新采集，不只增加发现条目。每组采集就绪即检查，分别记录原生执行、检查、排队和 agent 耗时；单 PR 工作流不等待无关候选完成。
  76 
  77 首版达标时直接报告通过，不人为插入错误来制造修复案例。后续发现首轮视觉判断遗漏时保留其局限、追加有依据的审查，不能只展示最终通过结果。
  78 
  79 发现问题后回看相关要求和条件组合：同一根因可能同时影响布局、方向、可达性或状态。逐项查看实际证据后再归因，不能自动把一个发现复制成所有规则失败。交付前反向审查证据账本里的缺项；分别报告根因簇、明确违反的规则和未评估方面，避免“问题已经看见但关联验收没检查”或重复计数。
  80 
  81 通常先检查容器尺寸和约束，再检查字体换行、图标及颜色细节。依据实际原因选择修改位置，不为消除差异随意增加偏移量。
  82 
  83 一次工作默认最多进行 3 轮视觉修正；同一失败没有改善时重新检查定位、状态和期望。保留当前最好的可解释结果及未解决证据，不继续无目标重试。这个限额可由用户或项目约定调整。
  84 
  85 每轮使用新的输出目录。目标平台依赖改变后，该平台旧 capture 不能用于声明新代码通过；未证明的依赖范围保守失效。正式基准独立保存，capture/review 不更新它；新结果只有来自独立预期或已批准基准的支持才能成为通过依据。
  86 
  87 ## 在页面中复验并交付
  88 
  89 组件级检查通过后，用项目已有 UI/E2E 工具在实际页面检查本次相关的父容器、滚动、safe area、键盘、点击与状态切换。由截图工具验证的部分和由模拟器/设备验证的部分分别记录。
  90 
  91 输出修改位置、原生构建/测试执行情况、场景清单、逐项结论、实际图与参考图/差异图，以及尚未验证的内容。
  92 
  93 报告状态为 passed、failed、needs_reference、needs_review 或 error。视觉 findings 是模型判断；数值和像素结果是相应证据范围内的自动检查。不得把缺少测试、组件未执行、环境故障或缺少参考归入通过。

   1 # Independent design and generated assertions
   2 
   3 Use this for a new component or a material visual change. A design owner or a
   4 separate context fixes a typed reference before implementation. Give that context
   5 the requirement and approved design inputs; do not supply candidate code,
   6 implementation screenshots or a suspected defect as the answer. Ambiguous prose
   7 still needs design interpretation and review. Hashes cannot certify authorship or
   8 prove that a number captures the designer's intent.
   9 
  10 Keep the approval record and canonical lock SHA256 outside the implementation's
  11 editable check plan. In a repository this can be a reviewed design commit or a
  12 sealed experiment record. A changed design is a new revision with a new approval,
  13 not an adjustment to make a test pass. Existing approval remains valid; routine
  14 implementation does not need another permission step.
  15 
  16 ## Typed reference and contract
  17 
  18 See the complete neutral files in [design-example](../assets/design-example/).
  19 `reference.json` contains values such as `{ "value":10, "unit":"dp" }` at
  20 specific JSON Pointers. Each source record in `contract.json` declares its exact
  21 file SHA256. Source paths must stay within the independent design root.
  22 
  23 The contract fixes:
  24 
  25 - An owner, approval reference, complete rule IDs, logical targets and checks.
  26 - Scenarios, selected states, input/action indices and original stimulus routes.
  27 - Every rule × state cell, with both platforms' source expressions or a reason
  28   for N/A. No implementation-written per-state expected overrides are accepted.
  29 - Explicit comparison operators. `near` requires a separately sourced tolerance
  30   in the same native unit. Do not add tolerance to conceal an unexplained error.
  31 
  32 Expressions address `source` + `pointer`, or derive `add`, `subtract`, `multiply`
  33 from two source expressions. Addition/subtraction require identical units;
  34 multiplication needs a dimensionless source factor. There are no inline expected
  35 values, implicit pt/dp/sp conversions or executable source expressions. Colors
  36 require `#AARRGGBB`; events are ordered arrays of event strings.
  37 
  38 `bindings.json` maps each logical target to one exact native selector. It cannot
  39 contain expected values, operators or exclusions. It also includes logical action
  40 targets. The native action route must match the independently fixed route after
  41 selector substitution. Target resolution still requires unique actual native
  42 nodes; a correct source citation cannot establish a correct runtime binding.
  43 
  44 ```sh
  45 python3 scripts/design_contract.py seal --contract design/contract.json \
  46   --root design --output approved-lock.json
  47 # Record the printed canonical digest in the separate approval record.
  48 python3 scripts/design_contract.py compile --lock approved-lock.json \
  49   --lock-sha256 APPROVED_SHA --scenario public-normal --platform android \
  50   --bindings bindings.json --actions actions.json --output generated-android
  51 python3 scripts/native_checks.py --plan generated-android/plan.json \
  52   --observation run/observation.json --platform android \
  53   --design-lock approved-lock.json --design-sha256 APPROVED_SHA --output checks.json
  54 ```
  55 
  56 The compiler emits every applicable assertion, exact value/source/unit provenance
  57 and post-action targets. Execution reconstructs the plan from the pinned lock and
  58 rejects altered expectations, precision, state coverage, source claims or routes.
  59 Legacy hand-written plans still run, with `unverified_supplied_expectations` in
  60 their report; do not claim they received the independent source check.
  61 
  62 ## Style measurement scope
  63 
  64 `text_layout` reads actual available native fields, such as Android TextLayout
  65 `font_sp`, `font_weight`, `color_argb`, `lines`, `ellipsized`. Public iOS AX does
  66 not expose generic computed SwiftUI typography/color; those cells remain U.
  67 Visual review is a separate explicitly sourced `manual` assertion. Seeing pixels
  68 does not convert it into an exact native font/color measurement.
  69 
  70 `geometry` supports width/height and two-box `gap_x`, `gap_y`, `inset_left`,
  71 `inset_right`, `inset_top`, `inset_bottom`. The target is the measured box; the
  72 reference is the preceding box for gaps or containing box for insets. Choose
  73 `basis:semantic` or `basis:control` explicitly when the distinction matters.
  74 Design rules must declare `measurement:accessibility_box` or `layout_box`, or an
  75 `{ios:..., android:...}` map. Current collectors export public AX/Compose semantic
  76 boxes; `layout_box` remains U on both platforms. A SwiftUI text's AX frame can
  77 be much smaller than its `.frame` allocation. A Compose semantics modifier also
  78 does not independently prove the full layout box intended by a design.
  79 Both unique, fully visible boxes must occur in the same coherent native frame.
  80 These are observed edge distances in iOS pt or Android dp. They are not a proof
  81 of a framework's effective padding attribute, text glyph ink bounds or OS touch
  82 area. No cross-viewport subtraction is allowed.
  83 
  84 The collector schedules nearby unseen native boxes first and reuses each original
  85 viewport for all applicable targets. It preserves the original immediate callback
  86 interval, the global scroll budget and unavailable targets. Navigation estimates
  87 never become assertion facts; only subsequently captured native frames count.
  88 
  89 ## Report the remaining gaps
  90 
  91 Separate source rejection before acquisition, incorrect target binding, native
  92 style contradiction, visual judgment and unavailable measurement. Include all
  93 required U/N/A cells and the approved digest. An independently wrong typed design
  94 can still generate consistently wrong code and tests; only the independent design
  95 review and separate acceptance oracle address that remaining semantic risk.

   1 # Contract-bound visual observations and shared acceptance
   2 
   3 Use this when visual findings must be auditable against an independent design.
   4 The approved base contract still compiles through `design_contract.py`. Each
   5 manual rule checks one aspect, rather than a sentence combining colors, wrapping,
   6 hierarchy and state. See the complete [neutral example](../assets/atomic-example/).
   7 
   8 An independent owner pins a separate `policy.json` canonical SHA256 alongside the
   9 design lock SHA256 before implementation. It names each manual rule exactly once,
  10 its single aspect/method, and the component/host/system responsibility of its
  11 logical target. Visual Boolean propositions come from exact typed source pointers
  12 in the same sealed design, so `actual:true` answers an explicit approved question.
  13 Rendered color atoms use opaque source-backed ARGB. A canvas rule must specify
  14 which layer paints the sampled region; an ambiguous owner is a design issue to
  15 resolve before scoring. Hashes do not establish design correctness or authorship.
  16 
  17 ## Submit actual observations, not rewritten expectations
  18 
  19 ```sh
  20 python3 scripts/review_contract.py catalog --plan generated/plan.json \
  21   --design-lock approved-lock.json --design-sha256 LOCK_SHA \
  22   --policy policy.json --policy-sha256 POLICY_SHA --platform ios \
  23   --output catalog.json
  24 ```
  25 
  26 The catalog provides opaque stable `review_id` identities, original assertion IDs,
  27 targets, explicit propositions, generated expected values/units/operators and
  28 source provenance. Save the catalog digest from its `catalog_sha256` field.
  29 
  30 The reviewer submits only:
  31 
  32 ```json
  33 {
  34   "version": 1,

  65 facts for targets that cannot fit in one viewport. All frames must have the exact
  66 same native input/process/event binding. Input drift, wrong state/action, torn
  67 image/hierarchy captures and conflicting values remain U.

  71 absence without complete native full-content coverage. An oversized target may

  76 enabled/selected native facts are separate observations; a label after a blocked

  83 repair while their distinct state checks remain visible. A later shared repair
  84 does not retroactively establish that another state was reviewed or detected.

 101 Native CGRect doubles keep their exact represented rational values. The fixed
 102 `native-rect-pixel-cell-enclosure-v1` model constrains positive integer rectangle
 103 possibilities through every edge and the original width/height. Exact full-width
 104 raster comparison selects a unique integer translation; geometry is never
 105 rounded into a different style value. Whole-state repeated identities and their
 106 native order must remain compatible. Terminal coverage requires the executed
 107 native sentinel hittability guard and actual contained root-member geometry.

 109 Fallback also requires a pinned `coverage_domain_ref` and `coverage_provider_ref`.

 127 complete sweep with no match anywhere. An observed duplicate establishes native
 128 nonuniqueness and is not cancelled by a later viewport containing one match.
