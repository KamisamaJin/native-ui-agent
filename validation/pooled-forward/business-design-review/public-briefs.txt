   1 # T2 — Appointment rescheduling offer
   2 
   3 PUBLIC — initial PR implementers may read this directory.
   4 
   5 Deliver `AppointmentReschedulePanel`, a reusable additive AlertToast public SwiftUI View and Reply production Compose component. Use the exact typed API in API.md. Caller data is immutable; production owns actual UI decisions, state, and replacement transitions. `onEvent(String)` forwards local request payloads only.
   6 
   7 ## Business rules
   8 
   9 1. Begin with no visit mode, time, or attendee-notification approval. In person is available; Video is available only when allowVideo is true. Modes are single selection.
  10 2. Times are displayed in caller order. A time is enabled only when it is available and supports the selected mode. Choosing another mode preserves the selected time only if that time remains available and compatible; otherwise it clears the time. Choosing a time selects exactly one. Any actual mode/time change clears notification approval.
  11 3. Move appointment requires a selected available compatible time and Notify the attendee selected. The summary uses 'Video'/'In person'/'No visit mode', then ' · ', then the selected time label or 'No time selected'.
  12 4. Successful submission emits exactly one 'reschedule|{entityId}|{in_person or video}|{slotId}', then locks the offer. All other decision taps emit zero events.
  13 5. Same-entity replacement preserves an allowed chosen mode and a still available compatible chosen time; invalid choices clear. Notification approval and recorded status always clear. A changed entityId clears mode, time, approval, and recorded status, including reused slot IDs. Replacement emits no callback.
  14 6. The component requests an appointment move locally; actual calendar mutation and app navigation are outside scope.
  15 
  16 ## Layout and style
  17 
  18 The annotated initial/ready SVGs are independent structural and qualitative references, not candidate renders or cross-renderer glyph pixel oracles. Native tokens in reference.json are normative. Order: heading, entity, description, prerequisite choices, dependent option rows, summary, status, final review/approval, primary. T4 puts its current preview between Preview notice and wording approval. Credit/modes/intake checks precede their dependent choices. Every section participates in one eager vertical flow. Keep all supplied text, selected decisions, disabled options and their reasons visible through natural wrapping; no ellipsis, internal scrolling, overlay, or horizontal overflow.

  20 The panel is opaque white,16pt/dp inset,16pt/dp radius. Option rows use12pt/dp inset,8pt/dp gaps/radius, and an empty trailing24pt/dp paint gutter. Major sections use16pt/dp gaps. Selection marks are leading. Primary fills available content width, has48pt/dp control-box height and8pt/dp radius, with its fixed centered short label. Selected rows retain selected fill when recorded state locks controls; unselected disabled rows use disabled fill. Enabled unselected rows are white. Primary uses its separately sourced enabled/disabled opaque fills; do not blend native disabled alpha over them.
  21 
  22 iOS uses explicit system22pt semibold heading and16pt regular body intent at Large Dynamic Type. Android reuses Reply titleLarge22sp/600 and bodyLarge16sp/400. iOS typography/layout/ink questions are qualitative source-backed atoms; no generic computed font, color, layout-box or padding extraction is claimed. Android available TextLayout font/color facts are separate native atoms. The48pt/dp primary assertion measures its actual control accessibility box, not physical OS touch area.

Disabled choices are assessed as native facts without pretending to activate them. A repair in one state never proves another state's evidence.
