## What the certificate can establish

The certificate establishes a continuous chain of captured rendered viewport
strips from the actual top through the actual terminal boundary of ONE admitted
closed main-scroll state, at the captured raster resolution. For an independently
approved finite immutable single-scroll domain, this can establish its full
rendered main-scroll coverage without treating an estimated total height as truth.

It is not an unconditional proof of all logical content in arbitrary UI. Finite
screenshots and AX observations cannot exclude an unobserved conditional branch,
a background insertion behind the scan, an unexposed nested/off-axis region, an
occluded layer or a changing data source. Stable PID/input/event records alone
cannot rule those out. Such claims remain U unless their separate domain and
state premises are independently established. A certificate never proves style,
native font/padding, physical hit area, callback correctness or business acceptance.

### Closed-domain admission premise

Before using this strategy, independently pin the root/state, source/dependency/
input/environment identities and a finite immutable content-generation contract.
The domain must be a single outer vertical flow at the approved width, with
trustworthy native top and terminal boundaries, no unaccounted occluding/sticky
layers, nested/off-axis content or live/asynchronous mutations during the sweep.
Lazy realization may refine unused aggregate-size estimates; it may not change
the actual admitted content or shift the observed overlap into another state.

The sealed neutral fixture meets this *declared domain* shape: a finite 18-row
view, one host-owned outer vertical scroll, marker/detail supplied only by the
fixed fixture input, and state/callback changes only through the approved actions.
Its authored component has no asynchronous content source. That is a precondition
about the pinned controlled fixture, not a conclusion from these screenshots.
Actual installed source/bridge/host identity and runtime bindings still must match.

Future business use needs an independent design/source owner's explicit approval
of the finite closed main-scroll state, or a real provider establishing equivalent
content-generation immutability and boundaries. Do not infer it from a successful
probe, source hash alone, a passing height test or repeated screenshots. Unproved
live, nested, hidden or off-axis scope remains U. Hidden branches requiring an
action need their own selected state/evidence; no union across states substitutes.

## Required observable certificate

Every condition below is required. A failed/unavailable condition is U for full
coverage, with its raw reason. There is no voting, best subset, relaxed numerical
threshold or fallback to the last reported height.

### 1. Original, coherent state-local frames

Use an ordered sweep of the exact approved state/checkpoint, with original raw
PNG attachments, hierarchies, native viewport/mapping metadata and independent
file digests. Every member must have the same source/build/bridge/input identity,
input index, App/test session/process, event revision and actual environment for
that state. Native before/after-capture bindings and the existing epoch/geometry
coherence checks must hold. Retain all raw identities, not a claimed flag alone.

Do not join another state's footer, a target-only frame from an unverified scope,
an old screenshot, a different process/input revision or a torn image/hierarchy.
PNG dimensions, color encoding and the native-to-PNG mapping must be compatible.
The actual viewport is a recorded image rectangle; no arbitrary image strip may
be discarded to improve agreement. A non-integral/unsupported mapping stays U
unless an independently fixed provider encoding supports it without interpolation.

### 2. Native leaf witnesses for each adjacent link

Each adjacent pair needs at least TWO independent native leaf witnesses:

* Actual positive-size native leaves, fully inside the measured scroll viewport
  and image in both frames, belonging to this scrolling content root.
* A stable native identity already exposed by the real view/provider, uniquely
  resolved in each original hierarchy. Label/role/value/state fingerprint and
  native ancestry must agree. A recycled index/path, repeated label or coincident
  wrapper is not identity. Never add or guess a business identifier to claim one.
* Distinct leaves with non-overlapping vertical witness boxes. A parent and child,
  duplicated semantic/control wrapper, two coincident labels or one large
  partially visible item cannot supply two witnesses. Zero/one witness and a
  single overlapping support band are sparse and cannot establish a link.
* All eligible common visible scrolling leaves must support the same motion.
  Do not choose two convenient leaves while discarding another contradictory
  identity, attribute, size, horizontal location, order or motion observation.

These requirements reject sparse/ambiguous local matches. Full-width raster
comparison below supplies dense support across the complete overlap, including
background between leaves; the two witnesses are not a replacement for that
dense test. If witnesses are insufficient, request bounded additional native
frames within the original sampling budget or retain U. No gap interpolation.

Native rectangles and their documented projection constrain a single vertical
translation; no scale, shear, rotation or horizontal drift is allowed. Preserve
original native point/pixel geometry. A raw floating displacement such as
653.0000000000002 is not an independently exact integer-pixel measurement merely
because it is close to 653. The integer raster translation must be established
by exact pixel registration and be compatible with the provider's documented
geometry representation. Lossless integer candidate search constrained by native
geometry is acceptable. Ad hoc epsilon, a tolerance chosen from this packet, or
rounding native geometry into a new design tolerance is not. Unsupported or
ambiguous representation remains U; native geometry/style rules are unchanged.

### 3. Unique, exact full-width raster overlap

For a downward step with pixel viewport size W×H, establish one unique integer
translation D with `0 < D < H`. The common strip is the entire W×(H-D) rectangle:
frame i rows `[D,H)` must exactly equal frame i+1 rows `[0,H-D)`, for every pixel
and channel. Its mapping must agree with all native witnesses. Preserve comparison
dimensions, original image hashes, decoded-strip hashes, mismatch count/bounds and
all candidate translations/rejection reasons.

Use a lossless, independently fixed decode of compatible original color/alpha
encodings. No resizing, interpolation, image registration warp, channel tolerance,
perceptual score, percentage-match allowance, OCR or masked pixel region is allowed.
A single pixel difference fails this link. Repeated textures yielding multiple
possible translations are ambiguous, even if one gives a pleasant mosaic.

Native geometry and pixels must agree; neither alone can rescue the other's
contradiction. A zero-motion repeat may be retained only as an exactly matching
duplicate and earns no progress/extra coverage. Stalling never establishes the
terminal boundary. Unsupported fractional-pixel motion retains U; it cannot be
resampled into agreement. All requested frames/conflicts remain in evidence.

### 4. A complete ordered chain and actual boundaries

Set the first registered offset to zero and accumulate the verified integer D
values. Each viewport strip has width W and height H; successive intervals
strictly overlap. This provides connected rendered coverage without using any
aggregate estimated height in the proof. Retain those height samples unchanged
for diagnosis, with their actual `stable_height:false` when applicable.

Require independently verified native top reach at the first frame and the actual
terminal boundary at the last: a terminal sentinel/last boundary tied by the
pinned host/domain contract to AFTER all admitted component content, fully
observed in the real scroll flow. Preserve raw native boundary geometry, root
membership and the existing native bottom-reach/hittability guard receipts where
the host contract requires them. `intersects_viewport`, a requested scroll, an
unmeasured hit field or a self-entered `bottom_reached` is insufficient by itself.
An overlay sentinel, an early sentinel, an unproved branch after it or an oversized
partial endpoint cannot close the domain.

All repeated leaf identities anywhere in the state must retain consistent
registered order, fingerprint and geometry under the same mapping. Revisits and
contradictory intermediate frames cannot be dropped. A broken adjacent link
cannot be bypassed by joining a more distant pleasing pair. If the complete
short domain is demonstrably inside one native viewport, direct native top/end
closure and its entire original image can prove that bounded case without a
multi-frame chain; the closure/domain requirements still apply.

### 5. An auditable result with narrow credit

Write a new coverage proof receipt that binds the original request/execution,
state, source/input/epoch, domain approval and all original native/image files.
Include strategy/version, frame membership/order, every native witness and
mapping, unique translations, exact raster comparisons, boundary receipts,
registered intervals and every unavailable/conflicting fact. The receiver must
recompute it from originals; a `complete:true` assertion alone is not evidence.

Report `proof_strategy: observed-strip-chain-1`, not `stable_height:true` when
that was false. Preserve the original numerical values and rule verdicts. Only
the admitted state's actual rendered main-scroll coverage earns credit; all
separate native/business/style/source responsibilities still require their own
evidence. This receipt does not override a failing native test, SDK error,
environment mismatch, action guard, source fingerprint or cleanup gate.

## Host-owned indicators and common foundation

Any right-hand scroll-indicator difference inside the viewport fails the exact
test. Do not label the strip harmless, mask it, crop the width, tolerate its
pixels or reinterpret the original failed packet as passing. A host-owned
indicator may instead be deterministically configured hidden BEFORE NEW captures
in both shared hosts, with configuration/template hashes pinned. The real
component, fixture width, content and expected values remain unchanged; verify
actual viewport/environment so hiding an indicator cannot silently alter layout.

The collector, its coverage verifier/acceptance consumers and any deterministic
host setting must be the same exact foundation bytes for baseline and candidate.
Record all changed files and before/after hashes. Keep original v12.6 intact;
create explicitly named derived baseline/candidate snapshots with the common
foundation revision, and freeze both before performance. Do not give one arm a
different pixel/anchor proof, host, timeout, sampling budget or native cache state.
All extra collection/registration/validation/retirement work is timed normally.

This addendum does not relax the old one-point height criterion or recertify that
legacy method for arbitrary UI. A stable-height proof may retain its original
implementation/threshold within its admitted stationary closed-domain scope.
It cannot override known binding, motion, boundary or content contradictions.
For a lazy-height state whose legacy proof is insufficient, this strategy needs
ALL the evidence above; missing evidence stays U. Taking the end/min/max height,
increasing epsilon or accepting an observed-ID union is not an alternative.

Reacquire the original exact five-state neutral workload under the common
foundation. All 104 rules, actual callback/update routes, full-state requirements,
mandatory native-gate judgments and advisory U remain visible. Do not alter UI,
expected labels, count, rows, design tolerances, requirements or hidden-branch
scope to make the collector succeed. No new business task begins on this basis.
The same-code/same-coverage ≥20% gate and exact P/F/U/no-increased-mandatory-U
comparison remain unchanged, and no preflight failure is a performance sample.
