# Endpoint clarification before neutral native runs or business design

The preregistered consumer endpoint is the unit's own full public capture plus
automatic checks result. It is called verification-result latency. Where visual
MUST clauses await independent grading, it is not yet complete acceptance.
The later independent public acceptance disposition and grading closure timestamp
are reported separately. Do not assign the earlier automatic endpoint as an
accepted-PR time merely because later judges eventually agree. Research barriers
and hidden grading costs are explicitly separate from consumer execution latency.
A single-PR production total would include its required visual review, but the
cohort judging protocol does not independently measure that counterfactual total.
