# Frozen evaluation and cost method

The shared evaluator is the unchanged core acceptance.py, native_checks.py,
review_contract.py and native_pixels.py, frozen with the candidate before design.
Two independent visual panels answer each approved qualitative proposition from
original state-bound PNGs; agreement is not human gold. Native and pixel facts
cannot be voted away. Failures need two independent product-cause assessments;
observation/system/unknown are retained separately. Unsupported U never passes.

The unchanged previous generic metrics.py and metric controls are copied here
before designs. Original machine or manual alarms receive two original-evidence
claim judgments; schema errors are not false product alarms. TP only credits the
intersection of exact original scope and independently confirmed product failures.
Cluster membership needs independent agreement; no retrospective cross-state
expansion. Repeated states/platforms are manifestations, not independent bugs.

Each new task has exactly one initial dual-platform source, copied byte-exact to
both arms after source seal. Shared initial data, automatic evidence and optimized
execution infrastructure are equal. Exclusive v9 features are obligation routing,
source-backed execution selection and targeted retest decisions; native backend
speed is measured separately and never added to the exclusive blind quality effect.

Final public MUST facts and independently agreed visual facts determine release
status. Any required failure, missing route, incomplete capture, invalid environment
or U blocks acceptance. Advisory rules remain in coverage tables. Hidden FINAL
inputs assess generalization after the complete final/public cohort seal. All
external D0/FINAL grading and original claim/cause judgments seal before reveal.

Primary consumer endpoint: that unit's handoff timestamp to its own full public
raw + automatic checks result, queued work included. No waiting for unrelated arms
is intentionally added. Initial authoring, neutral engineering, external grading,
hidden collection, full experiment and publication/CI are separate. Same-code speed
requires equal required state/target/action/callback coverage and no extra U or known
status conflict. Record reversed order repeats, cache state and failures. Two task
pairs support descriptive paired values only, not a general population/P90 claim.
Tokens and monetary cost are unavailable unless actually reported by the host.
