# Prospective final miss and false-release interpretation

Before business designs, define the final public self-check comparison on the
EXACT same full native bytes used by independent panels. A known final false
negative is self-P against an independently confirmed MUST product-F. Self-U
against product-F remains unresolved: show both lower known-miss and upper
miss-with-U bounds. Self-F/product-F is a supported final detection. Self-F against
independent P is a known assertion false alarm; external U or nonproduct cause
stays uncertain. Report conditional false-discovery ratio; classical FPR stays
N/A because this is not the universe of possible product faults. Dual-AI consensus
is not human gold. Zero natural-failure denominators stay N/A.

A confirmed false release requires a full AI self-accepted result plus at least
one independently confirmed public MUST product failure. An independent-U release
is separate. Hidden FINAL failures assess unseen caller-data generalization and
are not retroactively counted as originally observed or as D0 repairs. The same
initial public failure population defines paired repair outcomes. New regressions
need independent D0-P to FINAL product-F on the same public row.
