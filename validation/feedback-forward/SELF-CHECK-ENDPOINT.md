# Additional prospective consumer self-check endpoint

Recorded before any new business design or initial PR. The original primary
automatic-result endpoint stays unchanged. Also measure a secondary actual full
Skill endpoint: after its source is irreversibly closed and fresh full public
captures return, the same repair agent inspects those final images and submits
all approved qualitative actuals through the common source-bound review service.
The service recomputes native/pixel/visual checks, coverage and environment, then
records per-unit completion and MUST gate status. No further source edits, SDK
requests or third repair loop are allowed. Visual review time and actual queue
remain part of this secondary consumer total; it is not an external truth claim.

Both conditions receive the same final-check service and strict independent
requirements, so this infrastructure is shared. All units' sources, full public
raw/automatic endpoints AND self-check endpoints must close before hidden or
external grading. Independent quality panels later audit final omissions and
false releases without feeding repairs. That separate research grading is not
silently charged to either treatment or presented as its exclusive benefit.

Report initial shared authoring + per-unit handoff-to-self-check as an observed
PR implementation/review cost. Design-authoring and research costs stay separate.
Only actual accepted self-checks receive a self-accepted latency, explicitly
labelled AI self-verification; independent acceptance disposition/time is separate.
The two-task cohort does not measure a population production/human acceptance time.
